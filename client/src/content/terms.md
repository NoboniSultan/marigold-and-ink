## 3. Commission Process

Every commission begins with an idea, and we want to make the process as clear as possible.

### A. Submitting a Request

To request a commission, you must have a verified account.

Our commission form may ask for:

- Artwork size.
- Preferred artistic medium.
- Number of subjects.
- Whether the artwork will be physical or digital.
- Whether you require rush delivery.
- Additional notes or creative instructions.

Submitting a commission request does not guarantee acceptance or confirm an order.

We reserve the right to review each request and determine whether it is something we can accommodate.

### B. Quotes and Pricing

After reviewing your request, we will provide a quote outlining the proposed artwork, price, and any relevant details.

Quotes are valid for **14 calendar days** from the date they are issued unless otherwise stated.

If a quote expires, we may require you to submit a new request or receive an updated quote.

A commission is not confirmed until you accept the quote and pay the required deposit.

### C. Deposits and Starting Work

A **50% deposit** of the agreed commission price is required before work begins.

The remaining **50% balance** is due after the final design has been approved and before the artwork is delivered or shipped.

We will not begin working on a commission until the deposit has been successfully received.

### D. Revisions

Each commission includes up to **two rounds of revisions**.

Revisions allow you to request reasonable adjustments to the artwork while it is in progress.

Examples may include:

- Adjustments to colors or small details.
- Minor changes to facial features or expressions.
- Small composition adjustments.

Revisions do not include completely changing the original concept, subject, or agreed-upon scope of the commission.

Requests beyond the two included revision rounds may require additional fees, which will be communicated before the additional work begins.

### E. Turnaround Times

Estimated completion times will be communicated during the quoting process.

Turnaround times may vary depending on:

- The complexity of the artwork.
- The number of subjects.
- The selected medium.
- The number of revisions requested.
- Current commission volume.

Any rush request must be approved by us before the commission is confirmed. Additional rush fees may apply.

Unless explicitly agreed otherwise in writing, all completion and delivery dates are estimates rather than guaranteed deadlines.

We will make reasonable efforts to communicate significant delays.

Because commissioned artwork is personalized and created specifically for each customer, our cancellation and refund policy depends on the stage of your order.

### A. Before the Deposit Is Paid

### C. After Final Payment

Once the final balance has been paid, the commission is considered fully paid.

- Personal preference after final approval.
- Minor differences between the approved design and the finished handmade piece.
- Incorrect expectations that were not communicated during the commission process.

However, if your physical artwork arrives damaged during shipping, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery and include photographs of the damage and packaging.

We will review the situation and determine an appropriate resolution, which may include a replacement, repair, or refund.

Any resolution will be handled in accordance with applicable consumer protection laws.

### D. Tree-Planting Contributions and Refunds

Giving back to the environment is an important part of Marigold & Ink.

For every fully paid commission, **25% of the full commission price is allocated toward tree planting through Digital Humani.**

Once a commission is fully paid, the associated tree-planting contribution is submitted as part of our environmental commitment.

Because this contribution is intended to support reforestation efforts, the amount already committed to tree planting cannot be refunded to the customer.

If a refund is approved after the tree-planting contribution has been submitted, the contribution will not be reversed or reimbursed.

This applies to both full and partial refunds, subject to any rights you may have under applicable law.

### E. Payments for Cancelled Commissions

If a payment is received for a commission that has already been cancelled, the payment will be automatically refunded through the original payment method.

## 6. Our Commitment to Giving Back

At Marigold & Ink, we believe creativity can contribute to something bigger.

We dedicate **25% of the full price of every fully paid commission** toward supporting tree planting through [Digital Humani](https://www.digitalhumani.com/).

Here's how our giving commitment works:

- A commission must be fully paid before its tree-planting contribution is submitted.
- We calculate the contribution based on 25% of the commission's full price.
- The contribution is used to request trees through Digital Humani's reforestation platform.
- Tree quantities are rounded up to the next whole tree.
- Contributions are associated with commission references rather than customers' personal information.

Digital Humani connects participating organizations with reforestation projects and planting partners.

Our intention is to make every commission contribute to a greener future.

To learn more about our environmental commitment and why we chose Digital Humani, visit our **[Rooted in Something Bigger](/giving)** page.
